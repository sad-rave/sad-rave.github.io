the xcentrics

not the best film in the programme (that was Irome by Hiroyuki Oki - an incredibly perfect and beautiful film object), but the biggest surprise was The Eccentrics by Jennifer Burfond.

seeing this the same week as the kinet screenings, it felt way ahead of its time in a way i couldn’t quite explain. looking up the director later and seeing she made films with/about breer, a lot clicked into place. really exciting work, sat there on the lightcone website for viewing with about 15 other works. not as exciting as this one, but great work that seems to have been missed.
